Formula 01 • Base Chemistry

The "Sheer Milky Pink" Semi-Opaque Base

The number one mistake clients make is requesting a clear base. For high-end editorial French sets, request a semi-translucent cool milky pink rubber base that camouflages free-edge discoloration while preserving natural light reflection.

Salon Tech Dialogue:

"Please use a semi-sheer milky pink rubber base (two thin coats). I want 60% opacity so the free edge is diffused but the nail retains a translucent glass finish."

Formula 02 • Tip Geometry

Deep C-Curve Smile Line Ratio

Straight-across French lines shorten the finger visually. To elongate your nail bed, specify a deep smile line where the white arc hugs the sidewalls before tapering to a fine tip.

Salon Tech Dialogue:

"Almond shape with a crisp deep smile line. Please bring the wings up high along the sidewalls and keep the center tip line under 1.5mm thin."
