Formula 01 • Structural Integrity BIAB Architecture

Architectural BIAB & The Apex: Structural Balance for Natural Nails

Builder in a Bottle (BIAB) is more than just thick gel—it is structural engineering for the natural nail plate. By placing a concentrated droplet of viscous self-leveling builder gel exactly at the upper third of the nail bed (the stress zone) and flipping the hand upside down for five seconds, the apex aligns organically with gravity. This prevents bending fractures without bulky artificial thickness.

Curated Base Pigments & Hex Codes
Milky Nude BIAB #EAD8CD
Translucent Shell #F3E6DE
Natural Foundation Pink #DBC4B8
Recommended Silhouette

Tapered Natural Almond or Medium Oval

Directs impact shocks toward the reinforced apex center.

Curing Technique

Low-Heat Mode (99s LED/UV)

Prevents exothermic heat spikes on sensitive natural nail beds.

Verified Salon Request Script

"Almond shape on natural nails. Apply a thin slip layer of flexible rubber base, followed by a structured bead of sheer milky BIAB builder gel to build a gentle natural apex. Invert hand for 5 seconds to level, flash-cure, and finish with a high-shine glass topcoat."

Tech Advisory: Rebalance recommended every 3 to 4 weeks to keep the apex positioned over the natural stress zone.

Formula 02 • Clinical Hygiene Dry E-File Mastery

The Russian Dry Manicure: Micro-Clearing the Eponychium Pocket

Traditional water soaks expand the natural nail plate with moisture, causing premature gel lifting once the nail shrinks back. The dry Russian technique uses specialized diamond flame and sphere e-file bits at 10,000–12,000 RPM to lift dead pterygium without contacting living tissue. This allows gel color to be applied directly beneath the skin line for two weeks of seamless growth.

Prepped Base Pigments & Hex Codes
Bare Rosewater Tint #F8E7E3
Clean Cuticle Neutral #F1DCD5
Natural Keratin Pink #ECD1C8
Recommended Tooling

Red Grit Diamond Flame & Silicone Polisher

Soft grit prevents heat abrasion and eponychium scarring.

Under-Cuticle Coverage

0.5mm Micro-Tuck Under Proximal Fold

Creates the illusion that the manicure grows out naturally.

Verified Salon Request Script

"Short natural squoval nails. Full dry apparatus manicure (Russian e-file technique with fine diamond flame bit). Lift and trim dead cuticle skin only, dehydrate thoroughly, and tuck a sheer tinted rubber base 0.5mm beneath the proximal fold."

Tech Advisory: Only allow licensed certified technicians experienced with e-file safety to perform proximal fold exfoliation.
Formula 03 • Optical Chemistry Wet Glass Finish

The Liquid Glass Plumping Topcoat: Optical Refraction & Zero Shrinkage

Ordinary topcoats thin out as they cure, resulting in micro-ripples and dullness after a week of exposure to domestic friction. High-index non-wipe plumping formulas utilize photo-initiators that cure in an ultra-dense molecular matrix. The finish acts like a convex magnifying lens over pigment, intensifying shade depth while resisting hair scratches and dullness.

Reflection Swatches & Optical Codes
Liquid Glass Reflection #FFFFFF
Wet Lens Sheen #F5F5F7
High-Index Gel #EAEAEA
Lamp Cure Duration

Full 60 Seconds (Never Under-Cure)

Under-curing causes immediate surface clouding and chemical softness.

Edge Capping

360-Degree Free Edge Seal

Prevents moisture infiltration that triggers micro-peeling at the tip.

Verified Salon Request Script

"Almond shape. Seal entire design with a heavy-duty plumping non-wipe glass topcoat. Ensure the free edge is fully capped, allow the gel to self-level for 4 seconds before lamp entry, and cure for a full 60 seconds without early removal."

Tech Advisory: Let nails rest for 60 seconds after leaving the UV/LED lamp before applying cuticle oils to lock in diamond brilliance.
